      How the position is estimated
      
      
        
        
      1. Take the newest event in the feed: the train arrived at or left a station, and when.
        2. 
        
      2. Move the train along that section at the timetable's pace since the event.
        3. 
        
      3. Never let it pass a station the feed hasn't confirmed. Hold it just short and mark it as approaching.
        4. 
        
      4. Widen the error band the longer the feed stays quiet. Carry the delay onto every later stop, and never leave a stop before its timetabled departure.
        5. 
      
      
      
      Not handled yet: timetable slack that lets late trains catch up, real track geometry from OpenStreetMap, and passing-station reports. Real feeds include those reports, and they would shrink the error band a lot on long sections like Kota to New Delhi.
