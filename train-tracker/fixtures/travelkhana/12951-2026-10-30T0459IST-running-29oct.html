Overview of 12951 Mumbai Rajdhani Express

Want to get an insight of the superfast running Mumbai Central - New Delhi Rajdhani Express, running with train number 12951? If yes, do read on the following and you will get to know of the same for sure. Belonging to Western Railways, Mumbai Central - New Delhi Rajdhani Express Runs in between New Delhi and Mumbai Central in India. Train 12951 runs from Mumbai Central to New Delhi junction across the country and runs on daily basis. The distance travelled by the train in total is 1384 km. The operating speed of the train is 91 km/hour and the travel time is of around 15 hours and 42 minutes.

Do you wish to know the train timing as well at which it leaves and reaches the desired junction? In accord to the time table, train 12951 leaves from Mumbai Central at 17:00 hours and reaches New Delhi at 08:35 hours next day. It stops by at 6 halts in total in between the journey and they are at Borivali, Surat, Vadodara, Ratlam, Nagda and Kota. The train has 20 coaches available in total including AC first class, AC 2 tier and AC 3 tier coaches. There is a pantry car attached within and if desired, do feel free to try out e-catering food meal services from Travel Khana.
